Aviation Safety Disclaimer

Advisory information only

Maps, station dots, weather overlays, METAR/TAF decoding, SIGMET/SIGWX displays, NOTAM summaries and airport opening information may be delayed, incomplete, inaccurate or unavailable. Cached information can remain visible after it becomes stale. Check the original source, issue time, validity, area and applicable time zone. Map and airport data are not for navigation.

HOT and winter operations

Holdover-time estimates and countdowns are reference aids, not authorisations to take off and not a determination that an aircraft is free of contamination. Results depend on the selected fluid, concentration, weather, precipitation intensity, temperature and start time. Verify the current approved tables, all limitations, actual conditions and required inspections. Fluid manufacturer instructions and your operator’s procedures prevail. A timer, notification or Live Activity may be delayed, suspended or unavailable.

Calculators and temperature correction

Fuel, density, time and temperature-correction results depend on entered units, assumptions, reference values, elevations and temperatures. A nearby weather observation may not represent conditions at the aircraft. Independently verify results against current approved aircraft performance and procedure data. No calculator result establishes adequate fuel, obstacle clearance or regulatory compliance.

Scratchpad, requests and exports

Scratchpad drawings and locally saved values are convenience aids and are not certified records. Rapid Request prepares a draft; check its registration, services, quantities, recipients and timing before copying or sharing. Generating, copying or sharing a draft is not confirmation of delivery, acceptance or service completion.

Official sources take precedence

Official aeronautical publications, current weather and NOTAM briefings, aircraft and fluid manufacturer documentation, regulations, and your operator’s approved manuals and procedures always take precedence. Do not use the app while doing so would distract from safe operation.

Controller and scope

Rasmus Sundsten (rasmus@sundsten.fi) is the developer and contact for privacy questions about Stations, also called Globe Stations. This policy covers the native app and its embedded web content. External websites and data providers have their own policies.

Data stored on your device

Settings, theme preference, station pins and recent selections, cached weather and web data, calculator inputs, tool state, scratchpad drawings, and the terms version and acceptance date can be stored locally. Aircraft registration and service details entered into Rapid Request are used to prepare your draft. Core use does not require an account. Do not enter confidential information you do not want stored on the device or included in a draft.

Location and device permissions

With your separate iOS permission, location is used to position the map and support nearby-station and weather functions. Relevant coordinates or map areas may be sent to a provider when a location-based query requires them, including external reference lookups. Location access is not permission to advertise or track you across apps. You can deny or revoke access in iOS Settings and still use manual station lookup. Optional motion-related features use device permissions where required.

Network requests and third parties

Fetching weather, NOTAMs, satellite/radar imagery, map resources and aeronautical reference information sends requests to the selected providers. They receive your IP address, request time and technical connection information, and may receive the station identifier, map area or coordinates needed for that request. Providers include AviationWeather.gov, IPPC/Avinor, FAA services, national AIP sites, NASA GIBS, EUMETSAT, LibreWXR and other sources selected by the feature. Opening the public legal page contacts GitHub Pages. These providers may keep their own logs and process information in other countries under their policies; we do not control their retention.

Sharing and exports
