Stations — Privacy Policy
Version 2 · effective 30 October 2026

Controller and scope
Rasmus Sundsten (rasmus@sundsten.fi) is the developer and contact for privacy questions about Stations, also called Globe Stations. This policy covers the native app and its embedded web content. External websites and data providers have their own policies.

Data stored on your device
Settings, theme preference, station pins and recent selections, cached weather and web data, calculator inputs, tool state, scratchpad drawings, and the terms version and acceptance date can be stored locally. Rapid Request remembers the last-used aircraft registration and up to eight recent registrations locally, so you can select them again. Registration and service details are used to prepare your draft; the recent list is not uploaded or shared automatically. Core use does not require an account. Do not enter confidential information you do not want stored on the device or included in a draft.

Location and device permissions
With your separate iOS permission, location is used to position the map and support nearby-station and weather functions. Relevant coordinates or map areas may be sent to a provider when a location-based query requires them, including external reference lookups. Location access is not permission to advertise or track you across apps. You can deny or revoke access in iOS Settings and still use manual station lookup. Optional motion-related features use device permissions where required.

Network requests and third parties
Fetching weather, NOTAMs, satellite/radar imagery, map resources and aeronautical reference information sends requests to the selected providers. They receive your IP address, request time and technical connection information, and may receive the station identifier, map area or coordinates needed for that request. Providers include AviationWeather.gov, IPPC/Avinor, FAA services, national AIP sites, NASA GIBS, EUMETSAT, LibreWXR and other sources selected by the feature. Opening the public legal page contacts GitHub Pages. These providers may keep their own logs and process information in other countries under their policies; we do not control their retention.

Sharing and exports
Copy, share and export actions provide the selected draft or document to the clipboard, the app or recipient you choose. That content may include registrations, service details, weather or notes. Review it before sharing. External apps, mail services and websites process it under their own policies. Stations does not automatically send Rapid Request drafts.

Diagnostics, advertising and tracking
The app does not include an advertising or cross-app tracking SDK in this release. We do not sell personal data or use it for marketing profiles. Apple may provide crash or diagnostic information according to your device sharing settings; such reports may contain technical device and app details and should not be assumed to be anonymous. Information you choose to send when requesting support is used to respond and investigate the issue.

Purposes and legal bases
Data is processed to provide requested app functions and maintain local preferences, on the basis of performing the app agreement where applicable. Optional device access uses your permission. Necessary security and reliability processing may rely on legitimate interests, subject to applicable law. Legal obligations may require certain records. Reading or acknowledging this policy is not blanket consent to unrelated processing.

Retention and deletion
Local data remains until overwritten, cleared through the relevant feature, or removed with the app. Settings → Clear all cache removes cached weather and web data but keeps preferences, pins, saved tool values, scratchpad and the acceptance record. Clear a scratchpad through its own controls; remove the app to remove its local container. Device backups, exported files, clipboard contents and third-party copies may remain separately and must be managed through those services. Provider logs follow the provider’s policy. Support correspondence is kept only as reasonably needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations.

Security, rights and children
We use platform protections and secure connections where supported, but no storage or transmission is guaranteed secure. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and a right to complain to a data protection authority. Contact rasmus@sundsten.fi; local-only data may need to be managed on your own device because we cannot access it remotely. The app is intended for aviation users and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact
This policy is version 2, effective 30 October 2026. Material changes to the legal bundle require renewed in-app acknowledgement. For privacy questions or requests, contact Rasmus Sundsten at rasmus@sundsten.fi.
